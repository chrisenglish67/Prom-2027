Elegance Under the Stars

Dress Code Visual Guide

Prom is a formal black-tie optional event. Please adhere to the guidelines below to ensure smooth entry.

Tuxedos & Suits

Dark or midnight navy suits, tuxedos with dress shirts, dress shoes, and formal ties or bowties are highly encouraged.

Gowns & Dresses

Floor-length evening gowns or elegant formal cocktail dresses. Metallic, celestial, midnight blue, or shimmering accents fit the theme perfectly!

Accessories & Details

Formal footwear required (dress shoes, heels, or embellished flats). Corsages, boutonnieres, and star-themed jewelry are welcome.

Prom Night Dress Code Policy

  • No athletic wear, denim, casual sneakers, or t-shirts.
  • Garments must remain secure and formal throughout entry and photos.
  • Guests from other schools must strictly follow the dress code guidelines.

Push / Pull Marketing Framework

Push Strategy (Outbound Awareness)

Direct outreach to actively inform students and parents about deadlines.

  • Morning intercom announcements & cafeteria posters.
  • Targeted Meta / Instagram Feed & Story ads.
  • Direct email broadcasts via student portal.

Pull Strategy (Inbound Engagement)

Creating excitement and organic desire for students to visit the website.

  • Interactive Song Request portal & Prom Court Voting.
  • Interactive Dress Code visual inspiration guide.
  • User-generated Instagram Story countdown stickers.
